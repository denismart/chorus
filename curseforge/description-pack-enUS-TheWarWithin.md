# Chorus: English voice pack — The War Within

English voice-over for **all The War Within quests**: 4,172 lines, 462 NPCs.

**Requires [Chorus](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/chorus)**: the core add-on that plays the audio. The CurseForge app installs it automatically.

Works with the **English game client** (enUS and enGB). Key characters speak in voices cloned from their in-game recordings: Xal'atath, Alleria, Anduin, Moira, Dagran, Brann, Gazlowe and many more. Other NPCs use voices matching their race: Earthen, Arathi, goblins of Undermine, nerubians, ethereals. The voice-over is AI-generated and is not official Blizzard voice acting.

Found a wrong word, a cut-off line or a voice that doesn't fit? Leave a comment or open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/denismart/chorus/issues/new/choose) with the quest and the NPC.

# Chorus: английская озвучка The War Within

Озвучка **всех заданий The War Within** на английском: 4172 реплики, 462 NPC. Работает с **английским клиентом игры**. Для русского клиента есть [русская озвучка](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/chorus-the-war-within-russian-voice-pack).
